# Pre-Neolithic Eras and Revolutions

## Problem / Context

BlueMarble's first playable era will be set before the Neolithic revolution. The tech tree
splits history into eras with "revolution" gates between them (hunter-gatherers → Neolithic
revolution → agrarian society → industrial revolution). This note covers what comes **before**
the Neolithic. It answers three questions: which eras and thresholds researchers describe,
whether anything precedes the Paleolithic, and how to word these in game text without overstating
disputed claims.

The design proposal built on this note is
[Era Tech Tree: Before the Neolithic](../../design/era-tech-tree-pre-neolithic.md).

## Key Findings

- **The archaeological record starts with stone tools.** Lomekwi, 3.3 Ma, is the oldest
  accepted site, and the Oldowan dates from about 2.9–2.6 Ma. Before that there is no
  archaeological era, only hominin fossils (Sahelanthropus about 7 Ma, Ardipithecus,
  Australopithecus). A pre-tool stage in a game is an inference, and the game should label it
  as one.
- **Czech names.** Czech Wikipedia uses *nejstarší / starý / střední / mladý / pozdní paleolit*,
  then *mezolit*. "Starší doba kamenná" means the whole Paleolithic, so do not use it for a
  sub-period.
- **The Neolithic "revolution" was not one event.** Childe coined the term in 1936. Cultivation
  attempts (Ohalo II, about 23 ka), flour grinding (Pavlov VI, about 29 ka) and bread (Shubayqa,
  about 14.4 ka) all predate farming. Game text should call gates *thresholds*, not single
  moments.
- **Gathering mattered as much as hunting.** Plant processing at Pavlov VI and in the Natufian
  argues against a "man the hunter" framing. The game should weight gathering equally with
  hunting.

## Evidence

### Eras before the Neolithic

| Era (EN / CZ) | Dates | Hominins | Defining technologies and behaviours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the Paleolithic (no Czech term) | about 7–3.3 Ma | Sahelanthropus, Ardipithecus, Australopithecus | Bipedal walking. Unmodified stones and sticks used as tools, by analogy with chimpanzees. Cut marks at Dikika (3.39 Ma) are disputed |
| Earliest Lower Paleolithic / *nejstarší paleolit* | 3.3 Ma – 600 ka (Central European dates) | early *Homo* | Oldowan flakes and choppers, butchering carcasses, cracking marrow, scavenging |
| Lower Paleolithic / *starý paleolit* | 600–250 ka | *H. erectus*, *H. heidelbergensis* | Acheulean hand axe (from 1.76 Ma), use of fire, wooden spears, fire-making with pyrite (Barnham, about 400 ka) |
| Middle Paleolithic / *střední paleolit* | 250–40 ka | Neanderthals, early *H. sapiens* | Levallois prepared cores, hafting and glues, regular hearths, burials, ochre, barbed bone points |
| Upper Paleolithic / *mladý paleolit* | 40–14 ka | *H. sapiens* | Blades; bone, antler and ivory work; eyed needles; figurines; fired clay; textiles; spear-thrower |
| Late Paleolithic and Mesolithic / *pozdní paleolit, mezolit* | 14 ka – 5500 BC (Czechia) | *H. sapiens* | Microliths, bow and arrow, dugout canoes, nets, dogs; Natufian sedentism and bread in the Levant |

[cs: Mezolit](https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezolit),

Czech Wikipedia gives *pozdní paleolit* as "10 000–8 000 let", which contradicts its own
Mesolithic start of 9600 BC. Do not quote that figure.

### Thresholds described as "revolutions"

| Threshold | Date | What changed | Evidence and controversy |
|---|---|---|---|
| First stone tools | Lomekwian 3.3 Ma; Oldowan about 2.9 Ma | Access to meat and marrow | Oldowan: strong. Lomekwian: accepted, but few sites |
| Acheulean hand axe | 1.76 Ma | Tools shaped to a planned form | Strong |
| Use of fire | Wonderwerk 1.0–1.79 Ma; Gesher Benot Ya'aqov 790–690 ka; regular at Qesem 382–200 ka | Warmth, protection, cooking | Using fire is not making it, and many early claims may be natural fires ([Control of fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_of_fire_by_early_humans)) |
| Making fire | Barnham, UK, about 400 ka (hearths plus pyrite brought from elsewhere) | Fire on demand | New (Nature, December 2025). The makers are probably early Neanderthals ([NHM](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/news/2025/december/earliest-fire-making-dating-back-400-000-years-unearthed-in-suffolk-england.html)) |
| Levallois prepared core | about 400–250 ka | Planning several steps ahead | Probably invented more than once ([Levallois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levallois_technique)) |
| Hunting spears (Schöningen) | about 300 ka, re-dated to about 200 ka in 2025 | Planned group hunting | The new date is contested ([Mainz](https://press.uni-mainz.de/age-of-schoningen-spears-revised-to-200000-years/)) |
| "Cognitive" or Upper Paleolithic revolution | about 50–40 ka | Symbols, art, new tool kits | **Disputed.** The gradualist view points to Blombos (70–160 ka) and sees a "mosaic of traits" ([Behavioral modernity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_modernity)) |
| Projectile weapons | Possible arrows at Sibudu 72–60 ka; oldest surviving arrows at Stellmoor 11–9 ka; spear-thrower 17.5 ka | Killing from a distance | Early bow claims rest on wear on stone points ([Archery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_archery), [Spear-thrower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spear-thrower)) |
| Sewing | Possible needle at Sibudu 61 ka; Potok Cave 47–41 ka | Fitted clothing, life in cold climates | Moderate ([Sewing needle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewing_needle)) |
| Dog domestication | Undisputed: Erralla 17.5 ka; human–dog burial at Bonn-Oberkassel 14.5 ka | Hunting partner, guard | Earlier "Paleolithic dogs" (Goyet, Předmostí) are disputed ([Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestication_of_the_dog)) |
| Pottery before farming | Dolní Věstonice figurines 31–27 ka; Xianrendong cooking pots about 20 ka | Fired clay, boiling food | Strong; pottery is not a Neolithic marker |
| Broad-spectrum revolution | Flannery 1968: about 15 ka (Middle East), about 12 ka (Europe) | More small game, fish, birds, shellfish and plants in the diet | Debated, but the core idea holds ([BSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_spectrum_revolution)) |
| Trial cultivation | Ohalo II, about 23 ka | Wild cereals grown with weeds | The authors call it "trial" because it did not continue ([EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/767265)) |
| Natufian sedentism | 15–11.5 ka | Villages, sickles, bread | Strong ([UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2018/jul/bread-predates-agriculture-4000-years)) |

### Czech and Central European sites usable as content

- **Dolní Věstonice** (about 26 ka): mammoth hunters, a mammoth-bone fence, thousands of
  fired-clay animal figurines, a triple burial with red ochre and textile impressions. The Venus
  (31–27 ka) is among the oldest ceramics in the world
  ([site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doln%C3%AD_V%C4%9Bstonice_(archaeological_site))).
- **Pavlov / Pavlovian** (29–25 ka): needles, flutes, the earliest weaving impressions, and
  cattail starch on a grinding stone at Pavlov VI ([PNAS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20956317/)).
- **Předmostí** (24–37 ka): burials in mammoth bone and canids, one with a bone in its mouth.
  Whether these were dogs is disputed.
- **Stránská skála (Brno)**: Lower Paleolithic layers dated to about 600 ka, and local Jurassic
  chert quarried by the Bohunician (50–35 ka). A natural quarry node
  ([Archeologický atlas](https://www.archeologickyatlas.cz/en/lokace/slatina_bm_stranska_skala)).
- **Bečov** (Písečný vrch): a quartzite source and the largest Acheulean site in Czechia
  (about 300–250 ka).
- **Kůlna and Pekárna caves**: Neanderthal and Magdalenian layers; horse and reindeer hunting;
  bone points; an engraved rib.
- **North Bohemian sandstone rockshelters** (Okrouhlík, Pod Zubem, Pod Křídlem; 9–7 ka): Mesolithic
  hearths and pits, and tool wear dominated by plant processing.

## Implications for Design

1. **Pre-tool prologue.** An "Era 0" without tools works as a short prologue (walk, gather by hand,
   scavenge). The game must present it as inferred, not documented.
2. **Gates are thresholds the player shows by doing.** First knapped flake, then shaped biface,
   then making fire and hafting, then blades and needle, then bow and microliths, then the
   Neolithic threshold.
3. **Fire takes three steps.** Use natural fire, then keep a hearth, then make fire with pyrite
   or marcasite. Each step can unlock different activities.
4. **Pottery and dogs belong before farming**, but dogs only as "wolf companions (debated)"
   before about 15 ka.
5. **Gathering and hunting carry equal weight.** Plant processing (roots, cattail flour, nuts)
   belongs in the early eras.
